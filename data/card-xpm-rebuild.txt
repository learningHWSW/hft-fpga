Against the recorded figures (behavioural array): 471.7 / 551.9 / 747.8 ns.
The minimum and maximum are identical and the mean differs by 0.2 ns, which
is one 322 MHz cycle spread over 70 samples. The headline wire-to-wire
number does not depend on which memory description was compiled.

The loaded figures are the same 14 / 27.2 / 64 CYCLES as Phase A; the ns
differ only because Phase B's core clock is 200 MHz against Phase A's 215.

TIMING, HONESTLY. The routed report says constraints are NOT met: ap_clk
(clk_kernel_00) is -0.102 ns with 31 failing endpoints, all of them inside
u_capture -- the harness's HBM write master, 6 logic levels and 86% routing,
which is congestion rather than depth. Two things about that:
  * the DATAPATH clock met timing. ap_clk_2 closed at +0.030 ns, and every
    number above is measured on that clock or on the CMAC's.
  * Vitis auto-scaled the failing clock from 300 to 291.1 MHz
    (AUTO-FREQ-SCALING-04), so the shipped bitstream is timing-correct at the
    frequency it actually runs. It is not a bitstream shipped over a
    violation.

AND ap_clk IS NOT THE FREQUENCY THE MAKEFILE ASKS FOR. AP_CLK_B is 250 MHz;
cfgen received the request; the design was built at 300 and scaled to 291.1.
The harness therefore runs ~16% faster than the build claims. Benign here --
it met timing after scaling and the probes count other clocks -- but it is an
assumption the build has been making without checking.
