================================ RESULT ================================
wire-to-wire minimum, lat_probe, 322.265625 MHz (3.1031 ns/cycle):

  pair   near-end PMA      near-end PCS      difference
  1st    152 cy 471.7 ns   149 cy 462.4 ns   3 cy   9.31 ns
  2nd    153 cy 474.8 ns   150 cy 465.5 ns   3 cy   9.31 ns

Two independent bring-ups, both exactly 3 cycles. The absolute minimum moves a
cycle between runs -- it is the best of 70 samples and depends on which frame
catches the pipeline empty -- but the bracket does not.

CONTROL: the loaded probe (decode->order, inside the fabric, never crosses the
MAC) reads 14 / 27.2 / 64 cycles in ALL FOUR runs. If the difference above were
an artefact of anything other than the MAC path, this would have moved too.

  GT PMA round trip (serializer + deserializer + CDR + elastic buffer)
      = 9.3 ns

Against the MAC term measured in FINDINGS 7.6.0 (305.0 ns, of which ~19 ns is
our own fabric -- core-clock crossing, axis_sf_fifo fill, frame filter):

  GT PMA            ~9.3 ns    3 %    irreducible: any design driving a QSFP
                                      cage pays this
  CMAC MAC + PCS   ~276 ns    90 %    framing, FCS, 64B/66B, scramble,
                                      alignment markers, deskew, block lock
  ours              ~19 ns     6 %    already accounted for in 7.6.1

So the term a custom PCS/MAC would attack is ~276 ns, not the ~85 ns the
pessimistic case in PCS_MAC_SCOPE.md 3 imagined. The PMA is not the obstacle.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY. It does not say 276 ns is recoverable. A custom block
still has to frame, encode, scramble, lock and deskew; what it can drop is the
standards-compliant pipeline's worst-case provisioning, above all a deskew
buffer sized for the skew budget 100GBASE-R requires rather than the skew a
short link presents. This measurement bounds the PRIZE, not the winnings.
