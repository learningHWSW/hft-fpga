The 2023.2 column is what steps 1-6 silently used before the toolchain was
pinned, and is the tool behind every fMAX number in FINDINGS 7.6.x and 7.7.
The 2025.2.1 column is the pinned default -- the version the card is built
with, which is the reason to prefer it for a proxy of the card's timing.

Not worse on any directive. The netdly gain (+6.0) is large enough to matter:
it is the same size as the effects 7.6.2 and 7.7 reason about, which is why
those tables need re-running before a new build is compared against them.
